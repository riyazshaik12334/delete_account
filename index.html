Permanent Action

Deleting your account will permanently remove your report history, profile data, and notifications. This cannot be undone.

To delete your account, you can use the in-app option or send a request:

1

Open the Safe Roads Chennai app.

2

Navigate to Settings (renamed from Profile).

3

Tap on Delete Account at the bottom of the page.

4

Confirm your action on the following screen.

Alternatively, you can request deletion via email:
